# Mixdown

**Turn hours of footage into videos worth sharing.**

Mixdown is a Windows video editor for streamers, gamers, and creators. Go from raw recordings to finished videos and Shorts with a simple workflow built around the way creators work.

- **Find highlights** across recordings and replay clips.
- **Make your edits** with cuts, effects, captions, and control over individual audio tracks.
- **Create Shorts or full videos** with vertical and horizontal previews.
- **Collaborate** with editors, review changes, and organize publishing.

## Download

**[Download Mixdown for Windows](https://github.com/Znypr/mixdown-releases/releases/latest/download/Mixdown-for-editor.zip)**

Extract the ZIP and open `Mixdown.exe`. No sign-in is required to edit your own videos. Team features are optional.
